Privacy Policy for DentCase

Last updated: [Date]

Thank you for using DentCase. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

DentCase is a fully offline dental case management app. All patient data — including names, mobile numbers, age, gender, department, case notes, photographs, radiographs, billing details, and appointment records — is stored only on your device. We do not upload, transmit, or have access to any of your clinical data. No case information leaves your phone.

2. Third-Party Services and Data Collection

While we do not collect your dental case data, our app uses third-party services that may collect certain information for analytics and advertising purposes:

Google Analytics for Firebase

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the app. This service collects anonymised usage data such as:

  • App opens and screens visited
  • Device model and operating system version
  • General location (country level only)

This data is not linked to any patient or personal identity. It helps us improve the app. Firebase Analytics may also collect the Android Advertising ID to attribute analytics sessions. You can learn more about how Google handles this data by visiting the Google Privacy & Terms page.

AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)

We display advertisements through Google AdMob to support the app. AdMob may use the device's Advertising ID to serve interest-based (personalised) ads. In some regions (like the EEA and UK), you will see a consent form before personalised ads are shown. AdMob does not use any of your patient data. For more details, see Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

3. Permissions We Request

The app may ask for the following permissions, only to provide core features:

  • Camera: To take photographs and radiographs of dental cases. These images are stored only on your device.
  • Storage: To save and access the images you capture.

We do not access your camera or files without your explicit action within the app.

4. Data Security

The patient data you enter remains entirely under your control on your device. We recommend you protect your device with a strong password or biometric lock to prevent unauthorised access. Since we do not store your data on any server, we cannot be held responsible for data loss due to device damage, deletion, or theft.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You can delete any dental case, including all associated images and notes, directly within the app. All data is permanently removed from your device and cannot be recovered. If you wish to stop sharing usage analytics or advertising ID data, you can:

  • Opt out of personalised ads in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation).
  • Uninstall the app to stop all data collection.

6. Children's Privacy

DentCase is a professional tool for dental practitioners and is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date will be revised. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
